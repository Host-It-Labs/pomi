# Code of Conduct

Pomi contributors must be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment,
threats, discrimination, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not accepted
in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact
method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.
