# Pomi branding policy

“Pomi,” the Pomi logos and icons, official store listings, official domains,
and official package identifiers are controlled by William Guinaudie.

The intended policy permits accurate descriptive statements such as “a fork
of Pomi.” A distributed or publicly hosted fork must use a different product
name, icon, logo, package identifier, signing identity, and store metadata. It
must not imply endorsement or official status.
