# Governance

William Guinaudie is the project maintainer, release authority, license owner,
and final decision-maker. Community members may propose issues and pull
requests. Technical decisions prioritize security, privacy, maintainability,
and the product direction documented in `CONTEXT.md` and `docs/adr/`.

Only the maintainer may publish official Pomi builds, manage signing keys,
change the public license or branding policy, or approve commercial use.
