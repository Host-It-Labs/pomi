# Rebranding a fork

Before distributing or publicly hosting a fork:

1. Choose a name that is not Pomi and replace all logos and icons.
2. Change the Tauri identifier and Android/iOS/Wear application identifiers.
3. Use your own signing certificates and provisioning profiles.
4. Use your own domains, backend URLs, Firebase/APNs projects, feedback
   repository, telemetry projects, and AI provider metadata.
5. Remove wording that implies the build is official or endorsed.
6. Preserve third-party notices and the final Pomi license notice.

The public defaults use `app.pomi.community` only as a neutral development
identifier. It is not an official store identity and must be replaced before
distribution.

Subject to the final license, free binaries may be shared directly outside app
stores. App-store or marketplace publication and commercial distribution need
written permission from William Guinaudie.
